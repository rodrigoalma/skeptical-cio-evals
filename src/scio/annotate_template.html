      Keyboard shortcuts
      

        
p f
pass or fail (press again to clear)

        
↑ ↓
move between seller turns

        
x
mark or clear the first failure at the focused seller turn

        
n c
focus the failure note or the category

        
j k
next or previous call (arrow keys also work)

        
u
next unlabeled call

        
s
summary

        
Esc
leave a text field
